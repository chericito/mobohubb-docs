We have recently rebuilt our organizational structure levels for the Mobohubb portal to make it easier to search by region, site, or account. We have rebuilt our dashboards to allow everyone to see the dashboard data in the same way, for whatever roll up you have in place.

Roles are now color-coded, making it much easier to differentiate and identify them quickly.

![](https://files.readme.io/6973e5cc6467638f5be549089b109049a5cdb9f6c6d51956f842e202dd7c939a-image.png)

<br />

To add a new role, please follow these steps:

Go to General Settings.\
In the “Configuration” area, click on “Role.”
Look for the “Add new” button to the right of the interface; this button will be of the color chosen in the theme of the site. A form will be opened.
Fill in the details: Specify the purpose of the role by name.
Choose a color for the role from our color section.
Provide a brief description of the role.
If applicable, enter any payment-related information.
If applicable, enter any overtime-related information.
Check the “Activate” box.
Click the “Save” button to save your changes.

![](https://files.readme.io/ff7faa97b37cf7f6f4c9831dd4535549dbbd56c0f09be77755f50b4d0c3745d6-image.png)

Note: Visit our Roles section for more information.
