**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to log in (Mobile App & Web App)
* Scanning QR codes
* Managing the time clock and breaks
* Completing tasks
* Filling out forms or checklists
* Saving and retrieving drafts

***

# Log In

## Mobile App

1. **Enter your email**
2. **Type in your password** and tap on the **Login** button.
3. **Wait** for the login to complete.

***

## Web App

1. Access [https://webapp.mobohubb.com/](https://webapp.mobohubb.com/)
2. **Enter your email**
3. **Type in your password** and tap on the **Login** button
4. **Wait** for the login to complete
5. To see more options, **select the top-left button** with the three stripes.

***

# Scan QR Codes

1. **Locate** the landing screen.
2. Use the bottom menu and select the **Scan** option.
3. The **camera** will activate and scan the QR code.
4. **Submit** the QR code.
5. **Close** and validate. You can track your logs in **My Activity**.

***

# Manage the Time Clock

1. Access the footer menu and tap on the **Time Clock** icon.
2. Add a **note** for your check-in (optional).
3. **Hold down** the clock button until the green indicator completes (about 5 seconds).
4. Verify that the **stopwatch** is running.

**To check out** of the Time Clock:

1. Go back to the **Time Clock** section in the footer menu.
2. Add a **note** for your check-out (optional).
3. **Hold down** the clock button until the green indicator completes (5 seconds).
4. **Close** the time summary, where you can see the total hours worked.

### Manual Breaks

If the administrator wants breaks to be manual, you will see a **Take a break** button on the time clock. Tap it to choose from the **break options** configured by the admin (paid or unpaid).

***

# Complete Tasks

1. **Select** the task.
   * You can use the **search bar** at the top of the tasks section to easily find it.
2. **Swipe right** to mark it as done.
3. **Confirm** using the check button.
   * **Scan task**: Prompts you to scan a QR code or NFC with your device.
   * **Report task**: Redirects you to a form.
   * **Manual task**: Appears as a checkbox with no redirects.
4. Click **Close**.

***

# Forms or Checklists

1. **Select** the form or checklist.
   * Use the search bar at the top to find it quickly.
2. **Fill in the form**, noting the fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. **Submit** the form or checklist.
4. **Close** and confirm.
   * You can track your logs in **My Activity**.

***

# Drafts

### Saving a Draft

1. **Select** the form or checklist.
   * Use the **search bar** at the top to find it.
2. **Fill in** the form, keeping in mind required fields (\*).
3. Tap the **drop menu**.
4. Select **Save Draft**.
5. **Close** and confirm.

### Completing a Draft

6. Tap on **Saved Drafts**.
7. **Select** the draft form.
   * You can use the **search bar** to find it by the name assigned.
8. **Fill in** or complete the draft.
9. **Submit** the form or checklist.
10. **Close** and confirm.
    * You can track your logs in **My Activity**.
