SUPPORT DOCUMENTATION

CLAIM SHIFTS

​​Contents

It is possible to claim unassigned shifts, so users can claim them if they are published. This may require or not admin approval based on the schedule settings.

Claim shifts with no approval requirements.

It is possible to claim an open shift and have it available immediately if it is configured to not require any prior approval. These shifts once claimed will be displayed in your user.

To claim an unassigned shift, access to “Unassigned shifts to claim” in the schedule feature. You can access the Schedule using the footer menu.

Note: Remember you can use the upcoming shifts section, or the sliders section to access the Schedule feature. Visit our Schedule management to see more information.

1. Tap on “Unassigned shifts to claim.” This will redirect you to the unassigned shifts feature to see all you can claim.

This option won't be visible if you don't have unassigned shifts able to claim.

![](https://files.readme.io/ab0cb221a256dc5b08991fddb726ec6b08f9f5b66ef59b5f96b07d485d3ea0b0-679F6A02-8810-4521-BF79-DF05EFA9DD89.png)

<br />

2. Navigate through the “Unassigned shifts" section.

You will see the list of all unassigned shifts that you can claim. For each shift you will see relevant information:

Shift title: This title describes the shift.

Role: This is related to the role of the users who can claim it.

Date: You will see the exact date of the shift, and the duration. The total time that you need to work to complete the shift.

Number of tasks, scans or forms: You can see if there is any activity you may perform during the shift.

Location: This is the exact location you must perform the shift.

## Claim shifts with approval requirements

You can claim shifts, but they may need admin approval. These shifts once claimed and approved will be displayed to your user.

Tap on “Claim this shift” on the Unassigned shifts section.

When you claim an unsigned shift that needs admin approval, the “Claim this shift” button will change to “pending approval.” This will last until the admin approves the request.

![](https://files.readme.io/3e531c21fec56152d1c0bb3f6263aa743f30bdafb3beb515a7d29b4bcbc2ce13-FA54DC5E-19F8-4ADD-BA34-4901EFB20D14.png)

![](https://files.readme.io/6354e6cde3efcfdbdea175bbf540657e4881670019c5f7882540e4a1c4d2b9e8-8A0661C1-1042-4485-A3B4-6AC626DD542A.png)

<br />

If the user approves your request, you will see the shift in your schedule.

![](https://files.readme.io/faad0f0d3f52347ef4aba71b0a60fb720347f46181b6e2e6e6a9b7d36b1ccd91-32239021-F5D9-427B-98B9-7B6B4AE69113.png)

<br />

Note: The shift will disappear from the unassigned shift section and won't be displayed in your schedule if the admin rejects the request.
