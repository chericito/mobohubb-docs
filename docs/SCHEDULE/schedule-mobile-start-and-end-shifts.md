**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **start** a shift manually with no integrated clock
* How to **start** a shift using integrated clock (Time feature)
* How to **end** a shift manually or let the integrated clock end it automatically
* Where to **complete tasks** and activities during your shift

***

Shifts in mobohubb may be configured with or without an **integrated clock**. Depending on your organization’s setup, you can start and end your shift either by **Check-in/Check-out** or **Clock in/Clock out**.

***

## Start Shifts Without Integrated Clock

If your shift **does not have** the integrated clock feature:

1. **Open** the Schedule in the footer menu:

   * You can also reach it through the upcoming shifts section or the sliders on the dashboard.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/dd10a4cf51d88d4eab57ca85bb57ae496c7625d8fb7c0dcc9be0a4c1bb53283a-3189DF6D-7A4B-4CBB-B733-DAC3E7AFA936.png" />

   <br />

2. **Access** “Shift Details” via the **Planner**:

3. **Tap** **“Check in”** to start your shift:

   * Ensure you have already **accepted** the shift.
   * The work status changes from “Not Started” to **“Started”**.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/86b8fdf3be194032df2eb3108f4a8d70e6f73bfe6956d40f68a0240cf517c6e6-0CFF647A-0A77-4EE8-952E-CE0423E22DB9.png" />

4. **Complete** any assigned activities during your shift:
   * Tasks, forms, or scans as defined by your administrator.

***

## Start Shifts with Integrated Clock

When your shift **does have** the integrated clock feature:

1. **Open** the Schedule in the footer menu.

2. **Access** the “Shift Details” via the **Planner**:

   * Scroll down to view future shifts.
   * Tap on a shift to see its info.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/2403d04b4d57349d00048d417d82d8989b432de63e6b48623974a3f1505ef0a8-B7B0B475-89FA-415E-A34E-C1F288989B73.png" />

3. **Tap** **“Open timeclock.”**

   * Ensure you have already **accepted** the shift.
   * You’ll be redirected to the **Time** feature.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/44e932afbbc4f8dfbc7425b4e63b6f1c427f4cf18d89a776d42925d5df68afb2-B61F0EE5-1ED3-4877-92A8-ABAFF6C5C12D.png" />

4. **Clock In**:

   * The Time screen shows your shift details (start hour, shift name, color).
   * After clocking in, the shift’s work status changes from “Not Started” to **“Started.”**

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/bc2ccabc605d8794ab0113bdde4d4f91ddc8d5df3aa00d108ad406c225b558f0-6F0BB3CD-AF9E-47E8-944B-417BCD0314E3.png" />

5. **Perform** any associated activities (tasks, scans, forms) during the shift.

***

## End Shifts Without Integrated Clock

If your shift **does not have** an integrated clock:

1. **Open** the Schedule in the footer menu and select “Shift Details.”
2. **Tap** **“Check out”** when the shift ends:

   * The work status changes from **“Started”** to **“Completed.”**

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/7fd59c8d2a0da4911103121fd6322e382c40327eaa3c8608d87e597c400948f7-2ECFD078-6500-4FC1-86BC-3E981FC0C1CD.png" />

## End Shifts with Integrated Clock

If your shift is **integrated** with the clock, it may **end automatically** at the designated finish time, marking it **“Completed.”**

1. **Open** the Schedule → “Shift Details” to end the shift early if needed:

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/48346d902622468bce513bdb564f01c8762eff10dc4ff47a42e6d79d3c99e8a1-5EEF36A3-6FD5-446C-A7FC-6F9B52C77461.png" />

1. **Tap** **“Clock out.”**

   * This finalizes the shift manually before the set end time.
   * The work status changes from **“Started”** to **“Completed.”**

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/cb11f556657542d86bcf320e82d71364144bb95da89bcaa12553db688354967d-794E1AF9-57DD-4894-B888-AC61308E1E91.png" />

> **Note:** Once you’re clocked out (manually or automatically), the shift no longer appears as active. This ensures accurate logging of your work hours and status.
