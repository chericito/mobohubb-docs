SUPPORT DOCUMENTATION

REPLACEMENT REQUEST

​​Contents

It is possible to request a replacement for accepted shifts. This replacement should be done before the start time of the shift. Replacement requests are requested by users, and claimed by users, but they may need “admin approval” (if admin set the configuration) to successfully complete the request.

Make a Replacement Request.

Users have the option to request a replacement if they require another user to complete a shift that they are unable to complete. You can only have one shift replacement request active at a time. Access to “Replacement request” in the schedule feature. You can access the Schedule using the footer menu.

Note: Remember you can use the upcoming shifts section, or the sliders section to access the Schedule feature. Visit our Schedule management to see more information.

Tap on the accepted shift you wish to request a replacement; this will open the details of the shift.

1. Tap on the “+” button, then tap on the “Replacement request" button.

![](https://files.readme.io/bfee6c946b1554144b622e8124e0cbe1ea06827dcf8e44e84fb959aacd110751-6AD17B3D-F185-4BEC-BBB3-522969A9B39E.png)

<br />

2. Select the user who may accept the replacement request.

The list is filtered by users with the same role of the shift. You can only choose users from the selection.

Tap the user that you wish to see your replacement request.

![](https://files.readme.io/ebd4c4f4b0072eaa8a2c1c8e85a760a9398b440b96c04a40a02e3316c84e44b1-DF3E058B-1016-41CA-BC77-6EF86F66B588.png)

<br />

Tap on “Submit” to request a replacement to the shift, to the users.

![](https://files.readme.io/2e4b2a287b664b877d9feb82a07be252021d7e8b8f5cec87fe7df786b2c835b3-8CD2BAB2-C2B2-4EEB-B0C0-E887BBA8EFE2.png)

<br />

3. The Users will be able to accept and replace your shift or decline it.

![](https://files.readme.io/1c4bb7aef6c47b9192fd6bd75b8a3d2fbc81128b693e390ca19b972c36a52834-0AAEC14B-6140-4663-8584-97E0C04FDE12.png)

<br />

You will get notified when another user accepts or rejects your replacement request, and this will disappear from your user.

## Accept replacement request with no approval requirements

If you are available to finish another user shift that they request to replacement when they are unable to, you can choose to accept the replacement request. Access to “Replacement request” in the schedule feature. You can access the Schedule using the footer menu.

Note: Remember you can use the upcoming shifts section, or the sliders section to access the Schedule feature. Visit our Schedule management to see more information.

![](https://files.readme.io/3a1f626406ccbb727999b45c002f78f56896406fb8af18062003e93ea97815b7-7B70A9AD-9D19-4C93-926D-200A39659273.png)

<br />

1. Navigate through the “Replacement request" section.

You can scroll down the different requests. They are sorted by date in the calendar, showing the upcoming shifts first.

<br />

For each request you can see:

![](https://files.readme.io/af4bdc2b6fd4afcf0d527ace8f307b1cbfa7190abc70faeca10847fbadade34f-3C276FED-1B71-478A-827E-52961436DAB4.png)

<br />

The user who is making the request.

The start hour of the shift.

The duration of the shift.

The name of the shift.

The role associated with the shift.

The exact location of the shift.

2. Use the decision buttons to approve or decline request of replacement (shift).

![](https://files.readme.io/fef085c11f9a27a115bbe786644220fbca837c804aadf968755e310413a9d3a6-EEF718A5-6593-4310-8033-AACA65C5664F.png)

The shift will appear in your planner, as an “Accepted shift.”

## Accept replacement request with approval requirements

If you are available to finish another user shift that they request to replacement when they are unable to, you can choose to accept the replacement request. This may need approval from the admin. Open the replacement request in the schedule.

Note: Remember you can use the upcoming shifts section, or the sliders section to access the Schedule feature. Visit our Schedule management for more information.

![](https://files.readme.io/a827e517c58b2075a05fa93befff7264a3047bc8a686f743052d7a732c469e5c-62E05141-62CD-46E3-AAE9-3D210B59B780.png)

<br />

1. Navigate through the “Replacement request" section.

You can scroll down the different requests. They are sorted by date in the calendar, showing the upcoming shifts first.

![](https://files.readme.io/054693fc8d43c36894ce8d107605699fce4fef86b03c1ebcd66abae8f7688f28-116FF8B6-A229-4A35-88E7-DE3BEC4251B6.png)

For each request you can see:

The user who is making the request.

The start hour of the shift.

The duration of the shift.

The name of the shift.

The role associated with the shift.

The exact location of the shift.

![](https://files.readme.io/07230a8e2b556d49e3a49ae4b3a9a500dbf4028aa418b6b91b12ab10742a17af-FBA47985-5533-451E-95DD-F7AC4FFABD97.png)

<br />

2. Use the decision buttons to approve or decline the shift.

![](https://files.readme.io/fd7e06f9ea367e011f2dd34734d704335f3e9d9c236ec7794c6bd9716afb379c-776C18F4-4C11-4A5A-A457-6FD062D4A887.png)

When the admin approves the shift, you will get notified in your email, and it will appear in your planner, as an “Accepted shift.”

When the admin rejects the request, you will get notified in your email, and the request will disappear from replacement requests.
