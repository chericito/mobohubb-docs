Payroll is a sub-feature of Timesheet. It gives you a granulated view of each user shifts: clock ins, clock outs, hours worked, overtime, hourly rate and daily pay. Also, if you've assigned a recurrent shift or a one-day shift to a user, it can give you an esmitate of planned vs worked hours. The great value of this feature is that gives you lots of flexibility to modify hours worked, hourly payrate for each user, for each work/time period, easily filtered by using the Payroll filters: look at shifts and worked times from today, planned for the next days and older ones.

You can also approve, reject or leave on pending every single pay for every shift, making sure it complies with specific agreements or hourly payrates, overtime among other reasons.

And don't worry about missing out. With the audit log, you can see who and when made a change to any of the editable fields, giving you full transparency and control over your company payroll.

## Payroll by User

**Payroll by User** it's a general view for all your users payrolls. Use the filters to select a specific user/users and the date.

### Export

You can export your current view based on your filters by simply clicking the "Export" button right above the report, and the press "export data". You will get an excel file with the exact same view/information based on your filters. If you want a different export/view, you must change the filters configuration.
