## Add a New Role

To create a new role, follow these steps:

1. **Go to General Settings.**
2. **Navigate to the “Roles” section.**
   * In the **Configuration** area, click on **Role** to view, add, edit, and delete roles.
3. **Click on the “Add new” button** to the right of the interface.
   * This button will match the **color theme** chosen for your site.
4. **Fill in the details:**
   * **Role Name:** Define the purpose of the role.
   * **Choose a color** from the role color selection.
   * **Provide a brief description** of the role.
   * **Payment-related information** (if applicable).
   * **Overtime-related information** (if applicable).
5. **Click “Save”** to apply the changes.
