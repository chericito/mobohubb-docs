**In this guide you'll discover**

* How to log in to the new mobohubb portal with your existing credentials
* How to update your organization’s branding
* How to enable different login methods for your users (username/password, SMS code, email code)

***

## Welcome to the New mobohubb

We’ve introduced a fresh, modern interface aimed at streamlining your workflow and enhancing usability. While your favorite features remain, you’ll also find new ones to explore. You can continue using your **existing credentials** to access the updated portal.

![](https://files.readme.io/c92b3968a5028bb8577c2c6667d104b36a61c68f17278a16ece901f0106b895f-image.png)

<br />

When you log in, a **two-step walkthrough** will guide you through key configurations:

1. **Branding** – Reflect your organization’s identity.
2. **Users** – Update user information to enable flexible login options and ensure everyone can access the new portal or app.

> **Note:** All classic portal users (previously in “People” and “Portal Users”) have been merged into a new **Users** feature.

***

## Log in to the Portal (Admin Users)

1. Go to the **Mobohubb Portal** at [https://beta.mobohubb.com](https://beta.mobohubb.com).
2. Enter the **username/email and password** you used in the classic portal.
3. Click **“Sign in.”**
4. Once logged in, a **two-step walkthrough** will appear.

***

## Step 1: Update Your Organization's Branding

You can customize the new portal’s look and feel. The branding data from the classic portal has been imported, but you may need to **upload logos or icons** again if they weren’t transferred.

1. Click **“Branding.”**
2. **Review and update** your data:

   * **Logos, icons, and mobile logo** (these may require re-upload if not migrated).

   <br />

   * **Color theme** (Mobohubb defaults to green, but you can change it).

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/9fea9cc668b1978b0a2e6c6a8841d56deff5651d34ce7f20767267aabf733eca-image.png" />

   <br />

   * **Date and time format** (modify as needed for your organization).

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/f927efed137039763ac0d6a99d7b247fe2051d342136bae27f0a0bfd77898e20-image.png" />

   <br />
3. Click **“Save.”**
4. Check **“Branding”** so the setup indicator shows **1/2**.

## Step 2: Enable User Login Methods

In the **Users** section, you’ll find all the users who were active in the classic portal. Each user can choose their preferred login method for the **Mobohubb mobile app** (Android/iOS). To facilitate each login method, you must **update the corresponding user information**—email address, phone number, or password.

### Allow Users to Log In with Username & Password

1. **Click** on **“Users”**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/fe768788047de1bc742fc9ab608e1bea3eeed9ef99141f25aabdd8e6f4157df8-image.png" />

<br />

2. Under the **“User Name”** column, **click** the user you want to edit.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/c35a3b77904121fbb06a5987de27f8b25727e377f9065ebce394c1177adf3745-image.png" />

<br />

3. **Update the email address** field.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/46052edcc3ea1563f9d8f1beec54d9afde53384b7b51225957a8700c825ff163-image.png" />

<br />

4. Click **“Save”**. When you click “Save”, the portal will immediately navigate to the next tab and preload the information available to that user. If any information is missing, you can complete the essential fields with the necessary information. Also, while updating the user information, you may be able to grant new permissions to each user within the security groups. For further information about security groups and their functions, please watch this [guide](https://youtu.be/KjxaXNrGLdY?si=KLkvESW0xP_kczJy).
5. The portal navigates to the next tab automatically. If any info is missing, fill it in.
6. Go to the **“Settings”** tab.

<Image align="center" width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/7008d593a5ff57bc3afd7e5644e4a9c9508f2bb4d2559f3ac69bf649d09a8697-image.png" />

<br />

7. **Change the user’s password**, ensuring it meets the requirements of minimum 6 characters.

<Image align="center" width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/be82bb6091f59a247dc3d2868dae92a59a2b7de78de299ddf316c0c952dedea5-image.png" />

<br />

8. **Confirm** the new password.
9. Click **“Save and close.”**

> **Note:** Repeat for each user who needs username/password access.

***

### Allow Users to Log In via SMS

To enable **SMS verification**, update each user’s phone number.

1. Click **“Users.”**
2. Click the **username** you wish to edit.
3. **Update the phone number** field.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/637f0601098a2529b415b34e2ec363ad79c768d89c44e9752c5aed4873d6bd9f-image.png" />

<br />

4. Click **“Save.”**
5. If no further changes are needed, click **“Close”.**

> **Note:** Repeat for each user who needs SMS verification.

***

### Allow Users to Log In via Email Code

To enable **email verification** codes, ensure each user’s **email address** is correct.

1. Click **“Users.”**
2. Click the **username** you wish to edit.
3. **Update the email address** field.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/634b5526b03b787c5c7c78d64b19ebb566561dc1eeca2a0d8274bbd3acdd7b06-image.png" />

<br />

4. Click **“Save.”**
5. If no further changes are needed, click **“Close.”**

> **Note:** Repeat for each user who needs email code verification.

***

After updating **Branding (1/2)** and **Users (2/2)**, your new Mobohubb environment is **ready to go**. Check the final step to confirm the configuration indicator shows **2/2**.
