# Log in

1. Enter the web app with your credentials

![](https://files.readme.io/1b914f7e296fb01accbae435ef9bf9dc4c7bea16419597b6016f6daaa9d641b6-image.png)

## Select Your Site

You can have permissions on one site or multiple sites. If you have permissions or were assigned to various sites, the system will require you to select the site you're currently working on or the one you wish to submit a form about.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="60% " src="https://files.readme.io/2a540bdfa43e097ccc5b83653f93ce9fa0a1d74c9927233216df881c409aabbd-image.png" />

# Web App Navigation

After you select the site, you will get a general view of the Web App.

![](https://files.readme.io/9f0c73216c6ffc3aca035f32172faf8db345f0024fd6671147241180a369a75e-image.png)

In the center you will find the available forms to complete, as well as a search bar where you can filter by typing the name of other form when multiple reports are available.

![](https://files.readme.io/5172f035b383d4e84b11021a723a178f72011abeb8c92fdff5294a405c699b9c-image.png)

<br />

In the left you can click on the 3 lines to open a left panel, where you can find both forms and drafts navigation options. Your user information. And you can change your site by pressing the "Change Site" button, or you can sign out by pressing the "sign out" button.

![](https://files.readme.io/fef848c58d5d63ca9cf8f40222c068fba0c6606fd2efc4b55402e30d3009d25e-image.png)

<br />

And, on the right, you can click the Green button with the name of the current site to change sites.

# Forms

1. Select a form from the main screen.

![](https://files.readme.io/f50182c47b0a914beb7a806fa25ab0204e22c9352eba9d8d2c34db0fe19d5277-image.png)

2. A new screen will appear where you must fill in the information. Some of the fields or files buttons are mandatory to fill, based on the form settings. Mandatory fields/media files are marked in red.

![](https://files.readme.io/6e0e921b8e2787dd91f3f5ef9636858e5e82ded72d43c9b4aa7519a10cb6addb-image.png)

You can upload up to 1 audio clip, 10 images and 1 video file. You can also scan QR codes.

### Audio

![](https://files.readme.io/8db311948a93870fa7e2a55accbf2975890f9eeb34df043df0943ebf7c56c6d6-image.png)

### Images

![](https://files.readme.io/d75d7e965d82d377369a859e6cf4848755d94b3467cf4e4dc6b594349cb28169-image.png)

### Video

![](https://files.readme.io/8b079ba47d839b74275dd3ef72210f7d53d15e2805f5856ec94314eab507bd57-image.png)

### QR

![](https://files.readme.io/37dbaac620984db884bda95619c14b272c248dddd4cf92254edc2065a2841ece-image.png)

<br />

3. You can either save a draft or submit it using the buttons located at the right bottom of the screen.

   * Press "Save Draft" and assign it a name to save it to your drafts.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/1dd886ca50410c9f1af7f920c61170f24b9cb043dbb1ab7b56c230767d7a1363-image.png)

   <br />

   * Press "Submit" to send out the form completed to your supervisor or who may be interested.

# Drafts

If you decided to save a draft instead of submitting, you can find the draft in the 3 dots menu on the left-upper section of your screen.

1. Click on the 3 dots.

<br />

![](https://files.readme.io/5172f035b383d4e84b11021a723a178f72011abeb8c92fdff5294a405c699b9c-image.png)

2. Select "Drafts".
3. Select the draft you want to continue working on.

![](https://files.readme.io/138ea73e1b1d4c54dd7a8d772d5f746b6fa5c690a2bfb1a12fce412c812cf0c3-image.png)

4. Update or complete the information, add media files, etc.

![](https://files.readme.io/ab7c837c8bcc2822ac7d37b50048ffe7dca39ec4b0b60910ffd7408719d7b695-image.png)

5. You can:
   1. Delete the draft by Pressing "Delete Draft" from the buttons at the right bottom.
   2. Keep updating and saving the draft by pressing "Update Draft" from the buttons.
   3. Submit a draft by pressing "Submit" from the buttons.

And you're done!

This is everything you need to know about Forms and Drafts in the Web App!
