**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **sign in** to the **mobohubb** web application
* How to **recover** your password if you forget it

***

## **Sign In**

1. **Visit** [webapp.mobohubb.com](https://webapp.mobohubb.com/sign-in)

2. **Fill in** the **username** and **password** fields with your assigned credentials.

<Image align="center" width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/89a5918fe362bb3ee8130feaddc6bbcd7fb51c9b844c0bcfbbdd3fdb66e0ec18-image.png" />

3. **Click “Sign in”.**

***

## **Forgot Password?**

1. **Select** “Forgot Password?” on the login screen.

2. **Enter** your username and press **“Send Code.”**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="50%" src="https://files.readme.io/15960cf8bc7409328da36b41917bc4aad29805b8666ad568f150e7590e5f51c5-image.png" />

3. **Check** your email or SMS for the code (depending on organization settings).

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/686689c2c68b91b239cd4e8282e739061f128b652e4b4e9c65643c1a86f29637-image.png" />

4. **Enter** the code and your new password.

<Image align="center" width="50%" src="https://files.readme.io/5582e21793f129e896fba966843a3be6e20cac5aa722e41454b1418774f3b7a3-image.png" />

5. **Log in** with your updated credentials.
