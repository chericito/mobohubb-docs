We have recently **rebuilt our organizational structure levels** for the Mobohubb portal to make it easier to search by **region, site, or account**. Our **dashboards** have also been updated, ensuring that all users see data consistently based on their assigned **role and hierarchy**.

Roles are now **color-coded**, making it much easier to differentiate and identify them quickly.

## **Add Roles**

To create a new role, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the portal’s top menu.**
2. **Go to "General Settings".**
   * Click on **Settings** to access configuration options.
3. **Navigate to "Roles".**
   * Click on the **Roles** section within the **Configuration** area.
4. **Click on the "Add New" button.**
   * This button is located below the site administration menu.
5. **Fill in the required fields:**
   * **Role Name**
   * **Color** (choose from available options)
   * **Description**
   * **Pay Rate** (optional)
   * **Overtime Pay Rate** (optional)
   * **Activate or Deactivate Role**
6. **Save and Close.**

> **Note:** Once the role is created, it will be available for assignment in the **User’s Security settings**.

## **Edit Roles**

To modify an existing role:

1. **Navigate to "General Settings" > "Roles".**
2. **Find the role** you wish to edit (use filters if necessary).
   * Click directly on the **role name** to edit it instantly.
3. **Click the "Edit" button.**
4. **Modify any relevant details:**
   * Role Name
   * Color
   * Description
   * Pay Rate & Overtime Pay Rate
   * Activation Status
5. **Save and Close.**

***

## **Delete Roles**

To remove roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Select the roles** you want to delete.
3. **Click "Delete".**
4. **Confirm the deletion.**

***

## **Export Roles**

To export role data:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Click on "Export".**
3. **Choose the export option:**
   * **All Data**
   * **Current View Only**
4. **Download the Excel file.**

> **Note:** If the report file is too large, it will be available in the **Downloads section** of the platform.

***

## **Import Roles**

To bulk upload roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Click "Import".**
3. **Download the Excel template.**
4. **Fill in the template with role details.**
5. **Upload the completed file.**
6. **Confirm import.**

> **Note:** Processing time depends on the amount of data in the file.

***

## **Activate or Deactivate Roles**

To enable or disable existing roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Select the roles you wish to modify.**
3. **Click on the three-dot menu.**
4. **Choose "Activate" or "Deactivate."**
5. **Confirm the changes.**

> **Tip:** Check the **Role Status Column** to verify activation or deactivation.

***

This guide ensures efficient **role management** within Mobohubb, helping administrators **organize users, permissions, and responsibilities** effortlessly.
