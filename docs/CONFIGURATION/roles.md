We have recently **rebuilt our organizational structure levels** for the Mobohubb portal to make it easier to search by **region, site, or account**. Our **dashboards** have also been updated, ensuring that all users see data consistently based on their assigned **role and hierarchy**.

Roles are now **color-coded**, making it much easier to differentiate and identify them quickly.

## **Add Roles**

To create a new role, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the portal’s top menu.**
2. **Go to "General Settings".**

   * Click on **Settings** to access configuration options.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/2dc3cbf7f4a9f884677ad68145854ffe93be18becb6cceffda06df5a49b2e4f0-image.png" />

   <br />
3. **Navigate to "Roles".**

   * Click on the **Roles** section within the **Configuration** area.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/d998626aa4368ea4116a75ec1fdcb5ea83163357a6b5d110fbee9b878e48fffc-image.png" />

   <br />
4. **Click on the "Add New" button.**

   * This button is located below the site administration menu.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="70% " src="https://files.readme.io/3a9e45d28c95aaeb73d33be0b77448cd6c7dd0126634c07fd7964d649324f18f-image.png" />

   <br />
5. **Fill in the required fields:**

   * **Role Name**
   * **Color** (choose from available options)
   * **Description**
   * **Pay Rate** (optional)
   * **Overtime Pay Rate** (optional)
   * **Activate or Deactivate Role**

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/b0d84a56621643a959e8a04474dacc7133f9b9f925661ac5f7756cda3547ec94-image.png" />

   <br />
6. **Save and Close.**

> **Note:** Once the role is created, it will be available for assignment in the **User’s Security settings**.

## **Edit Roles**

To modify an existing role:

1. **Navigate to "General Settings" > "Roles".**
2. **Find the role** you wish to edit (use filters if necessary).

   * Click directly on the **role name** to edit it instantly.

   &#x20;

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/bbeac04a71863d695c009befe9999d72216381403c477f80810fccb20fca0d65-image.png" />
3. **Click the "Edit" button.**
4. **Modify any relevant details:**

   * Role Name
   * Color
   * Description
   * Pay Rate & Overtime Pay Rate
   * Activation Status

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/330e6c288a8bdde8006963ff2ed406c62a4bbf91c5d2d835583b40be26dfa7c9-image.png" />

   <br />
5. **Save and Close.**

***

## **Delete Roles**

To remove roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Select the roles** you want to delete.
3. **Click "Delete".**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/f3ac0f50bc345c9d17f1039cac9eb1344f6dac9a8b96c7526ddd986b99e2dca3-image.png" />

<br />

4. **Confirm the deletion.**

***

## **Export Roles**

To export role data:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Click on "Export".**
3. **Choose the export option:**

   * **All Data**
   * **Current View Only**

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/83579c6ca76bf06c653f20ed5dd3b168e1b17a0a4ea1c48b28af4ccaf2dfdfe1-image.png" />

   <br />
4. **Download the Excel file.**

> **Note:** If the report file is too large, it will be available in the **Downloads section** of the platform.

***

## **Import Roles**

To bulk upload roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Click "Import".**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5b9cd7acb14725012d3a5c88000b8eb2bd5f4447c60020ef14d5abc33a8905be-image.png" />

3. **Download the Excel template.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/49ee0faad6ef2095ba7a27398227e50654e1966826d51a1cd9755621a059bfbb-image.png" />

4. **Fill in the template with role details.**
5. **Upload the completed file.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/ff959386c0dc58c772f67cf6833b15dfdb159c1cb83773c2d816d9be7b2ff8ac-image.png" />

6. **Confirm import.**

> **Note:** Processing time depends on the amount of data in the file.

***

## **Activate or Deactivate Roles**

To enable or disable existing roles:

1. **Go to "Roles" in General Settings.**
2. **Select the roles you wish to modify.**
3. **Choose "Activate" or "Deactivate".**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/741d4f9fb8cd0b5991cd293fb4c4d2cbe44bd69e50a0eb209d4a75ffa901daa2-image.png" />

4. **Confirm the changes.**

> **Tip:** Check the **Role Status Column** to verify activation or deactivation.

***

This guide ensures efficient **role management** within Mobohubb, helping administrators **organize users, permissions, and responsibilities** effortlessly.
