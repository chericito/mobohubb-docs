# Definition

A **QR code** is a machine-readable code consisting of an array of black-and-white squares that can be scanned with a mobile device. In mobohubb, you can use QR codes to identify and manage your organization’s locations—such as sites, zones, or checkpoints—to streamline workflow and tracking.

***

## Ways to Generate QR Codes

1. **Order from mobohubb**
   * **Request** pre-printed and laminated QR codes directly from mobohubb (additional cost applies).
   * **Contact** mobohubb customer service or [create a ticket](https://mobohubb.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) on our website to place your order.
   * Perfect for organizations that prefer **ready-to-use** codes without manual setup.

2. **Use Templates**
   * **Download** the **Mobohubb**-provided templates in PDF format, organized by number ranges (e.g., 1–50, 50–100).

   * **Verify** that the QR codes in the file match the numbers you need.

   * **Print** the file on a high-quality printer for best results.

   * **[Download 200 QR Template](https://mobohubb.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/25709777445389)**

   * **[Download 2000 QR Template](https://mobohubb.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/25709791464717)**

3. **Create Your Own**
   * **Use** an online QR code generator to create codes for each location.
   * **Recommend** naming or labeling codes with consecutive numbers for easy organization.
   * Great for teams that want **full control** over the design and naming.
