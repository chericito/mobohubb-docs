A QR is a two-dimensional barcode that can be scanned with a mobile device to access information or perform actions. At Mobohubb, you can use QR codes to identify and manage your organization's locations, such as sites, zones, or checkpoints.

There are three ways to generate a QR code:

1. Order them from the company. You can ask Mobohubb to send you the QR codes you need, already printed and laminated, ready to use; this option carries an additional cost. To order them, you can contact us by email at Mobohubb customer service and specify your order details, or you can create a ticket on our website to contact you.

2. Using templates. You can download the templates provided by Mobohubb to generate QR codes for the locations you wish. The templates are organized by number ranges, such as 1-50, 50-100, etc. You will find them in a PDF file and will be asked to verify that the QR codes match the numbers you need. You will then have to print the PDF file on a high-quality printer.

3. Create them on your own using a QR code generator. You can create your own QR codes using an online QR generator; we recommend using the names of the locations serialized by consecutive numbers.
