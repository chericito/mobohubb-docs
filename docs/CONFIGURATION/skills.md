Skills refer to **acquired abilities** that allow individuals to perform specific tasks or activities **efficiently and accurately**. Unlike certifications, **skills can be developed or learned** through experience and training.

![](https://files.readme.io/6b180731cf6909d00290f09ecefae1240faa72635cf31d845b5b33df5ade0597-image.png)

***

## **Add a Skills Category**

To create a **new skills category**, follow these steps:

1. **Go to "Settings"**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/a9c7fc29bdfb656f126c675911e9fa75f0a928889951794cf15a5fbe4879314c-34426CED-2CC9-4B47-B83C-286E57C551DE.png" />

2. **Navigate to "Skills"** under the **Configuration** area.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/b70610f43b4f371eaf94dbaabb361ba4a6d5b458a7ecb8f1a0dde502be9e477d-5134B6AE-6AC5-42D3-97C8-2642E77018FD.png" />

3. **Click on the "Add New" button** on the right side of the interface.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/8c2ef4082073274356250cd2fb293748e35afce8fe06ccbb8661bdc001e27cb7-A62DC88C-DC86-42CA-A928-53117AB4858E.png" />

3. **Fill in the required details:**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/cf75daa57dfc8db870465b51ce555676ab19e5885a9e195a941efc8fda169322-2CC0891B-DE17-4A6C-99F5-49F2B3F9B588.png" />

* **Skill Name** – Specify the category name.
* **Description** – Provide a brief summary of the skill.
* **Activate/Deactivate** – Check the box to activate the skill. The skill will be active by default.

3. **Click "Save"** to confirm the changes.

![](https://files.readme.io/647c6fa004afa7235605b84ff4043c293dde9c59418d443a5fe7c1a19b2a9247-image.png)

## Export Skills

1. **Press** **"Export".**
2. **Choose** between:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/8d61cf0d57bb38b55fa9bca6e06d2dc41328f9a28bfae698655370ca6c7304d6-9336576A-C852-4CE4-A230-02BDAB11B1DE.png" />

* **Export Data**: Downloads **all** skills records in the system.
* **Export Current View**: Downloads **only** the filtered and column arrangement you currently see.

3. **Confirm** your choice to download the Excel file. If the file is large, it may appear in your **[Downloads](https://mobohubb.readme.io/docs/downloads#/)**.

## Activate or Deactivate Skills

1. **Select** one or more skills that share the **same status** (either all active or all inactive).

> **Note:** You cannot modify multiple skills at once if they have different statuses.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/e8afbedb4e9ce6db829940ad2229bbcbf75006514102dbf6445157d7702f94e6-619EB447-3ED9-4972-A56D-4E8E75C153CE.png" />

2. **Press** the **Deactivate** button if the selection is currently active, or **Activate** if the selection is inactive.

This approach keeps skill records consistent and makes it easier to manage employee abilities in **mobohubb**.
