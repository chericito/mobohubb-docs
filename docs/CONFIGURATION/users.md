***In This Guide You'll Learn***

* How to **add new users** using the portal
* How to **edit existing users** to update their personal information
* How to **activate or deactivate users** to control access

***

## Add Users

To add a new user:

1. **Navigate** to the portal’s top menu.![](NV94.png)

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/0868bad525a5345caba22115d8197fff8f766c69cad2b484c725b0d25f10273f-image.png" />

2. **Go to "General Settings."**

* In the top menu, click on **Settings**. This is where you will find various configuration options for your organization.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/9274db15b3806405299632386234188a0e4b979ee9624835b1e040d106c5e6b3-image.png" />

3. **Navigate to "Users."**

* Within the **Site Administration** area, click on the **Users** section.

![](https://files.readme.io/334df942d48c38141cd7767ac3b6407bd4a047c4bcba34a2dbecd1b8627723a1-image.png)

<br />

<br />

1. **Click** on the **"Add new user"** button in the user’s section.

![](https://files.readme.io/33497ed3a2ef7d8bed4ca80838b0d3885f46cfded116b313012ffc5cd036c93f-image.png)

<br />

1. **Enter** the new user’s data and **fill in** the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.

![](https://files.readme.io/e267ac639580be72da4c4088266f3e4c00461125e7b0fcef03304c2e8fd72bfc-image.png)

<br />

1. **Fill in** the "Phone Number" and "Email Address" fields.

   > **Note:** This email address will be the username.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/da63a3bc27f24c84cee07454c03d8eb7a063f7b7acce460287dad5a8684862d0-image.png)

   <br />

2. **Choose** the Security Group from the dropdown menu.\
   !\[]\(NV146 (7).png)

3. **Choose** between "Invite by SMS" or "Invite by Email" by checking the desired box.
   * When you send an email invitation, the user is expected to use their username and password to log in.
   * When you send an SMS invitation, the user is expected to use the SMS verification code to log in.
   * You may check both methods.
   > **Note:** If you choose "Invite by Email," fill in the notification email address. This can differ from the user's email.\
   > !\[]\(NV147 (2).png)

4. **Click** the **"Save"** button in the form to create the new user.\
   !\[]\(NV146 (1).png)

5. The person will receive an email or SMS (depending on their chosen method) with a link to confirm their registration.\
   !\[]\(NV145 (3).png)

> **Note:** Repeat the procedure for each new user.

## Edit Users

To edit an existing user:

1. **Navigate** to the portal’s top menu.\
   ![](NV94.png)

2. **Go to "General Settings."**
   * Click on **Settings** in the top menu (or use the side menu).\
     ![](NV95.png)

3. **Navigate to "Users."**
   * Within the **Site Administration** area, click on the **Users** section.\
     !\[]\(NV145 (2).png)

4. **Check** the checkbox for the user you wish to update.
   > **Note:** Only one user can be modified at a time. If more than one is selected, the edit option will not appear.\
   > !\[]\(NV145 (1).png)

5. **Click** the **Edit** button.\
   ![](NV147.png)

6. **Fill in** the empty fields or modify existing information in each section.
   > **Note:** There is a section with further details under User's Personalization.\
   > !\[]\(NV146 (3).png)

7. **Click** the **"Save"** button for each tab you want to modify.\
   !\[]\(NV146 (2).png)

8. **Close** the window by clicking the **"Close"** button.\
   !\[]\(NV146 (3) - copia.png)

## Activate or Deactivate Users

To activate or deactivate an existing user:

1. **Navigate** to the portal’s top menu.\
   ![](NV94.png)

2. **Go to "General Settings."**
   * Click on **Settings** in the top menu (or use the side menu).\
     ![](NV95.png)

3. **Navigate to "Users."**
   * Within the **Site Administration** area, click on the **Users** section.\
     !\[]\(NV145 (2).png)

4. **Check** the checkboxes for the users you want to modify.\
   !\[]\(NV178 (4).png)

5. **Click** the three dots button.\
   !\[]\(NV178 (5).png)

6. **Click** **"Activate"** or **"Deactivate"** depending on your needs.\
   !\[]\(NV178 (3).png)

7. **Confirm** the activation or deactivation:
   * For deactivation, a pop-up notification will appear; click **"Deactivate."**\
     !\[]\(NV178 (2).png)
   * For activation, a similar pop-up will appear; click **"Activate."**\
     !\[]\(NV178 (6).png)

8. **Check** the **user status** column to confirm the changes.
