**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to access **General Settings** and the **Configuration** area.
* How to add a new position using the **"Add new"** button.
* How to fill in and activate position details for proper organizational reference.

***

You can create positions in mobohubb and then associate them with the users you have created. These positions serve as **reference information** for your organization.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/2139149e7e7b3cf2715909bf80fd37c7c2322b75ca94f2164a2ef7bc1740ba7e-28ACD3FA-ED5A-4D70-9BDA-802737CFCE7F.png" />

To add a new position, follow these steps:

1. **Go to General Settings.**

2. **Navigate** to the **"Configuration"** area and click on the **"Position"** section.

3. **Click** on the **"Add new"** button, located to the right of the interface. A form will open.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/893755b75cef96078de62a2ad9947207b5b42a7d92c4cc96ac3beb93bdfe7c8f-72C4ACAF-EAB2-467F-8AB0-38D6F8C457DE.png" />

4. **Fill in** the details by **adding the position name**.

5. **Check** the **"Activate"** box.

6. **Click** the **"Save"** button to save your changes.
