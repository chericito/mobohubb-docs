**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **take photos** and **record videos** within the app
* How to **discard** or **confirm** your captured media
* How to **switch** between front and back camera modes

***

The mobohubb app offers an **intuitive camera interface** for capturing photos, videos, and scanning QR codes. Depending on your organization’s needs, you may be prompted to **verify attendance** for timeclock, and **attach photos** to forms.

***

## Taking a Photo or Video

When prompted to add a **photo** or **video**, the camera screen opens automatically:

1. **Camera Activation**

   * The camera will **launch** within the mobohubb app, typically for timeclock selfies, form attachments, or scanning.

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/97f57b667d4e6715c4f222ee5ab22238a03678b5d6196dac5460541d9afad3b5-Image_4.jpeg" />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/6a679e78e03050e236505798b231c859c1998f7d90779b92c1462045440052da-Image_5.jpeg" />

   <br />

2. **Capture Photo**

   * **Tap** the **round button** at the center/bottom of the screen to **snap a picture**.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/1d36acc1b35ddc39fbfc66257e61d590fd246cb43d5098402d67254bb5a5e4e4-Image_6.jpeg" />

   <br />

3. **Record Video**
   * **Tap** the **red round button** at the center/bottom to **start recording**.
   * **Tap** again to stop.

4. **Discard or Validate**

   * After capturing a photo/video, **tap** the **“x”** on the left to **discard**.

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/255b04a2362030ddee675b35cbee248b99dd8e070c1c380ca95bc5b7a61c5d72-Image_8.jpeg" />

   * **Tap** the **green check** on the right to **confirm** and attach it to the timeclock or form.

5. **Switch Camera**
   * **Tap** the **“change” button** in the bottom-right corner to toggle between **front** (selfie) and **back** camera before capturing media.

***

### Common Camera Prompts

* **Timeclock**: Take a **quick selfie** to confirm your identity when clocking in/out.
* **Forms**: Attach **supporting evidence** (e.g., photos of an issue or environment) to enhance form submissions.

***

### Finalizing Your Submission

Once you **confirm** the photo or video:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/e795f52f35c9e55c56762e6e0d6ad8525698a4d429958a75278b4ae684645ba6-Image_9.jpeg" />

* It becomes **attached** to your activity (e.g., timeclock log or form field).
* If it’s a **timeclock selfie**, mobohubb logs the media with your clock-in or clock-out event.
* If it’s part of a **form**, it’s stored with your form submission data.

> **Tip:** Double-check camera permissions in your device’s **settings** if you run into issues or can’t access the camera.
