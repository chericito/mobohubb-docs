**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to locate and use the landing screen and footer menu for scanning
* How to position your device correctly to scan an NFC tag
* How to submit the NFC tag and complete the validation process

# How to Scan NFC

A scan can be associated with a task or a form. The scan must be performed for the task/form to be completed. When you complete the scan, you will be redirected to finish the task or form as needed.

**NFC (Near Field Communication) tags** are small, passive electronic devices that store and send information wirelessly over short distances—typically a few centimeters. Each tag contains a microchip and an antenna that allow it to communicate with NFC-enabled devices, such as smartphones or tablets, when they are in close proximity.

***

## 1. Locate the Landing Screen

The landing screen is customizable by the portal administrator for each site. It can display options such as Scan/NFC, Dashboard, Forms, Tasks, Time clock, or Schedule. In this example, the dashboard serves as the landing screen, but the procedure remains the same regardless of the chosen screen.

***

## 2. Use the Footer Menu

The footer menu contains six options that give you access to different features (Scan/NFC, Dashboard, Forms, Tasks, Time clock, Schedule). Tap on **Scan/NFC** to proceed.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/fec7dd53f4a74121993ba2cc6ec0c0f485bce295409c90a5ceb65456d407222e-image.png" />

***

## 3. Get Close to the NFC Tag

Position your device within approximately 8 inches (20 cm) of the NFC tag to ensure a proper connection.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/3048a6fd01e56a479e13a5e3fe3ecd72b207392672849b32aeae4e08d2257f13-image.png" />

***

## 4. Submit the NFC Tag

If the NFC tag’s ID matches the scan point ID in the portal, the system will send the data. If it does not match, a message will appear indicating that the tag is not registered. In that case, you are allowed to register a new scan point.

***

## 5. Wait for the Validating Process

After submitting the NFC tag, the system validates the data. Please wait while the process completes.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/d51bb16e924971772d5bdebeaa78581f37c4faa6a0395e37cbe0028cd83d1be3-image.png" />

***

## 6. Close and Validate

Once validation is complete, close the process to finalize the scan.
