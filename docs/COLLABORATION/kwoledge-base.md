Mobohubb provides a central repository where users can upload and access all relevant **documents**, **policies**, and **articles**. The Knowledge Base is divided into **folders** (for organization) and **articles** (for specific content). You can add, edit, or remove these items as needed.

***

### **Add an Article**

1. **Open** the folder you just created.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/2940f74240029d8ec314bb7e5e9fd8ea5bfaac0c6b528fea6d83381c011efafe-image.png" />

2. **Click “Add New”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/bec44a36cc1aa777140f094d48ba7943718b7d7854141c3b911310cc34c6fc34-image.png" />

3. **Select “Create article”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/45c449e768f9ecc353eed20b352ccf6b315780d3d2d26b6bf8c2f7d3bab1f4ca-image.png" />

![](Captura%20de%20pantalla%202024-09-09%20082445.png)

4. **Fill in Article Details**

<Image align="center" width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/1be0548a7e7c207e0c414c08ec9011028f93d21e801ceff3137b4494ffb00b7b-image.png" />

* **Name** the article
* **Body** text (using the built-in style editor)
* **Images** can be inserted into the article
* **PDF files** (up to 25MB) can be uploaded for download using the "Select File" button.

5. **Save and Close.**
6. **Preview** the article by clicking on it.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="% " src="https://files.readme.io/a72c5f21ae9e280ad7a300b101421c900a17088e2338f1e23610411dca88b3fc-image.png" />
