The redesigned dashboard is divided into **global indicators**, **user activity**, **top forms**, **scheduled/completed tasks**, and a **planned vs. done** comparison. It also features new graphs that simplify data visualization. You can show data by **day**, **week**, **month**, or a **custom time range** using the GUI under the “Header” block.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/8446037da28b8b73b4f44711e0068f9f366c16753edfbbe391d1b35b5d724b68-image.png" />

***

## **Quick Filters**

Click on the corresponding option to filter by **day**, **week**, or **month**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/b833ccc0a3d309bb200587fd4760e04e175ff412f1acb209476d4b689c065a92-image.png" />

***

## **Custom Filters**

To select a **specific range**, click the appropriate option.\
You can also move both **start** and **end** indicators in the interactive calendar to define your custom date range.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/2213332c46b17794bf07aa0faa16130f4c271139c66321a3ccafe0121e88d183-image.png" />

> **Note:** The range you set will display in the **Global Indicators (GI)** area.\
> ![](https://files.readme.io/17cbcc9deab017a041e177dd3f1ff1a279791a3c8626ec6020f261c84e1e5fca-image.png)

***

## **Global Indicators**

Displays a bar graph showing totals for each indicator:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/f7a5fc45d55f27d8eaaaea2c9e541f81b6b0d6ae3583600d8569b648f06f4cf8-image.png" />

* **Users**: Total registered users in the system
* **Scan Points**: Total number of created scan points
* **Devices**: Total devices (phones, tablets, etc.) currently in use
* **Tasks**: Total tasks created
* **Forms**: Total forms created

***

## **User Activity**

Displays a line graph where each line represents a different activity:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/bab2a2719288ca5b809a9253f3d822309b688f02a51500966a70a0260be6e856-image.png" />

* **Forms Activity**: Number of form submissions within the chosen time range
* **Task Activity**: Number of tasks completed in the same period
* **Scans Activity**: Number of scan points scanned
* **Timesheet Activity**: Number of time clock entries made by users

***

## **Top Forms**

Shows the most frequently used forms and their submission counts. This helps identify which forms are most popular or critical:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/e70e2fcf1b2539f235403d7b321c886b8f8b99a3a3b29827994236f9483430d2-image.png" />

* **Forms**: Sorted by the highest to lowest submission counts

***

## **Schedule**

Provides various indicators for **scheduled** tasks:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/4b3b13e06e3dafadf9f52b5369a179897540b28e12f97d34cae3d38dfa40c736-image.png" />

* **Scheduled Shifts**: Upcoming shifts, including start and end times
* **Completed Shifts**: Shifts that have ended, showing actual start/end times
* **Total Hours**: Summation of hours worked across all shifts

***

## **Planned vs. Done**

Displays **4 groups** of two bar graphs each, comparing planned activities to those completed without exceptions:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/d4d574c6e71381848581c7553f25682ea5f46f31ee7a112e37527ab517e5c728-image.png" />

* **Timesheet**: Planned work hours (scheduled shifts) vs. actual hours logged
* **Scans**: Planned scan sessions vs. actual completed scans
* **Forms**: Expected form submissions vs. actual submissions
* **Task**: Assigned tasks vs. completed tasks

***

## **Not Complete Activities**

Shows a line graph highlighting the total **uncompleted** activities (marked as “No Activity Exception”) in a given time range. Each category is color-coded:

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5010332e598a6772606e52a17bc994c922dafc24f698ce8e7471bac716a6f8a6-image.png" />

* **Forms**: Incomplete mandatory forms
* **Task**: Tasks that weren’t finished
* **Scans**: Scan points not checked
* **Timesheet**: Missing time clock entries for required shifts
