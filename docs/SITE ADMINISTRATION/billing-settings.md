This guide explains how to access and manage the **Billing** section in **mobohubb**—where you can **change your plan**, **update payment information**, or **review payment history**.

## **How to Manage Billing Settings**

Only users with the **Billing** security group or **Organization Administrator** rights can access this tool. It redirects you to a **payment platform**, enabling you to update your mobohubb plan, payment method, and view your subscription details.

### **Accessing the Billing Platform**

1. **Go** to the portal’s top menu.![](NV94%20\(2\).png)

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/7cfdc6b9eb3801a502f826b0c351358b22121ae76266d977ed288fc928c433f9-image.png" />

2. **Navigate** to **“General Settings”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/0254487b5848471d256236210d485cbe9a66644464ada154399e57c0bc503abe-image.png" />

3. **Select “Billing.”**

* Within **Site administration**, click **Billing**.![](Imagen3.png)

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/b60e2ffe67dd18b157f5acb9727087a64fea2b8920d3bced58e92bea0cbcbf88-image.png" />

4. You will be **redirected** to the payment platform.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/a0cb7e693ff3a266076d0f71076e9273370cbcd82765c6e302775ea9351aeaf4-image.png" />

![](Imagen4.png)

## **Upgrade or Change Your Plan**

1. **Billing Platform**

   * Go to **“Current Plan”.**

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/82afd99cc5b0ffc091d4cf74fd5e0b36bfbef5c002ab8324cc93c10ed9a61da9-image.png" />

   <br />

2. **Click** “Change Plan”.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/5ec65bbf9509ae97e1144c1d2dc58504e92a65ea4a82813fa6e48218fdcd5c4a-image.png" />

3. **Select** your new plan options.

* Increase or decrease the **number of users**, switch from **basic** to **premium**, etc.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/85646359ee349fbabdf9cdb60d20fed846159630194cf74fc996e2421ad441bf-image.png" />

4. **Confirm**

* Review the **date**, **amount**, and **type** of subscription.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/288179110b3946e686d0a740972a0e5d5573bfd564c0aeb9bf9aefef66bb929d-image.png" />

* Click **“confirm”.**

***

## **Cancel the Plan**

1. **Billing Platform**

   * Go to **“Actual Plan”.**\
     ![](Imagen5.png)

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/1f5c7706c618d490588d6123d3fcc186e8442088041111011c05d66b4a79c74d-image.png" />

2. **Click** “Cancel Plan”.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/85e5ec6dcaeefedef934099f61696561fa420346a89e972461317c88eb063e64-image.png" />

3. **Confirm**

* The plan remains available until the **end of the billing period**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/662f8748b647bb18f4b0e257410e2ad67d72cc65c1eedfe41cb6bc9217701293-image.png" />

* Renew at any time if you change your mind.![](Imagen11.png)

## **Update Your Payment Method**

1. **Billing Platform**

   * Go to **“Payment Method”.**\
     ![](Imagen12.png)

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/defc89dc14eb5c3c38a60f2ae04a97ce660dfaa648a21b298e28717547d22944-image.png" />

2. **Click** **“Add Payment Method”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/42be812e7ead4c7c16ce923236ca216e60d3153c9f4777cdaa6f90c330890cac-image.png" />

3. **Fill Out** the form.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/c52aef34d1b035b9a4b00a936497920bcc7bccadcaaf1130f6ba293934be1abf-image.png" />

* Click **“add”.**

4. **Set the Default Method**

* Click the **3-dot menu** on the payment method to **make it default**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/cfdc7c73e47250b3a8311de4ec3436bbb60e22b0c72f925f1f9c457bd15ccbc5-image.png" />

> **Note:** You can remove other payment methods if needed.

***

## **Update Your Billing Information**

1. **Billing Platform**

   * Go to **“Billing Information”.**

   <br />

   <Image align="center" width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/81467bfa459633637fbe3208228934e1f0624754c33697ecb7b46ede75bcec95-image.png" />

2. **Click** **“Update Data”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/2466655509a12afedd5e6a04332063c9bf88de0fe95a174a32beb8c81908e919-image.png" />

3. **Enter** your details.

* Only the **email** is required; other fields are optional.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/10074a94a62ee1db902d157f7e253880f806a2324eee0d5bf6e27d47ff243fac-image.png" />

* Click **“keep”** to save.

***

## **Invoice History**

1. **Billing Platform**

   * Navigate to **“Invoice History”.**

Here, you can view the record of your **recent payments**, including amounts and dates.
