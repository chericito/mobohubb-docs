**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **access Site Management** and edit a site’s essential details
* How to **customize site branding** or inherit your organization’s branding
* How to **configure mobile app features** for each site (GPS polling, security, timesheets, etc.)

***

Site Management is divided into three main sections—**Site Information**, **Site Branding**, and **Mobile Configuration**—each accessible via a tab within the feature.

## Accessing Site Management

<Cards columns={2}>
  <Card title="Step 1" icon="fa-cogs">
    **Go to General Settings**\
    Find "Site Management" under "Site Administration."
  </Card>

  <Card title="Step 2" icon="fa-search">
    **Choose a Site**\
    Select your site from the list or use the search bar to find it.
  </Card>
</Cards>

### Parts

\<Tabs>
&#x20; \<Tab title="Site Information" icon="fa-info">

\*\*Site Information\*\* enables you to create and manage sites within your organization. Each site can be configured as follows:

\- \*\*Site Name\*\*: A unique identifier for the site.
\- \*\*Site Path\*\*: The address or directory where the site is located on the server.
\- \*\*Type of Site\*\*: Choose from security, facilities, janitorial, construction, restaurant, or “other.”
\- \*\*Site Address\*\*: The physical (or target) location of the site.
\- \*\*Time Zone\*\*: Determines how dates and times are displayed and recorded.
\- \*\*Enable Web App Access\*\*: Allows form access via \[https\://mobile.mobohubb.com/]\(https\://mobile.mobohubb.com/).
\- \*\*Activate the Site\*\*: Deactivate or activate the site without deleting it.
\- \*\*Type of Scan\*\*: Select \*\*NFC tags\*\* or \*\*QR codes\*\* for scanning.

\*\*How to Update Site Information\*\* &#x20;
1\. \*\*Select\*\* the site from the Site Management list. &#x20;
2\. In the \*\*Site Information\*\* tab, \*\*edit\*\* details like name, description, location, etc. &#x20;
3\. \*\*Save\*\* your changes to apply them. &#x20;

&#x20; \<Tab title="Site Branding" icon="fa-paint-brush">

\*\*Site Branding\*\* determines whether a site inherits the organization's branding or has its own design. Disabling “Inherit Settings” allows you to customize:

\- \*\*Description\*\*: A short text introducing the site’s purpose or value.
\- \*\*Logo app mobile\*\*: The site’s or organization’s logo in the mobile app.
\- \*\*Icon\*\*: Displayed in the top-left when the menu collapses.
\- \*\*Main logo\*\*: The organization’s graphic symbol on the web portal.
\- \*\*Date format\*\*: How dates appear (e.g., dd-mm-yyyy).
\- \*\*Time format\*\*: 12-hour or 24-hour display.
\- \*\*Theme color\*\*: The primary color for the portal’s interface.
\- \*\*Time zone\*\*: Adjusts time display relative to user location.
\- \*\*QR codes or NFC tags\*\*: Indicates the scanning method for the site.
\- \*\*Activate Site\*\*: Enables or disables the site.

\*\*How to Modify Site Branding\*\* &#x20;
1\. \*\*Select\*\* your site from Site Management. &#x20;
2\. \*\*Open\*\* the “Branding” tab. &#x20;
3\. \*\*Disable\*\* “Inherit Settings” if you want custom branding. &#x20;
4\. \*\*Adjust\*\* logos, date/time formats, theme color, etc. &#x20;
5\. \*\*Save\*\* your changes.

&#x20; \<Tab title="Mobile Configuration" icon="fa-mobile">

\*\*Mobile Configuration\*\* customizes how users interact with the mobohubb app at each site:

\- \*\*GPS Polling\*\*: Periodically queries device GPS for location tracking or attendance.
\- \*\*Security\*\*: Lets users stay logged in or requires re-authentication each session.
\- \*\*Timesheet Configuration\*\*: Records and manages time spent, including auto clock-out.
\- \*\*Landing Screen Configuration\*\*: Choose a default screen (dashboard, timeclock, tasks, forms, schedule, or scan/NFC). Up to four items can appear in the navbar; extras go under “Additional apps menu.”
\- \*\*More Menu Configuration\*\*: Show/hide items in the app’s side menu:
&#x20; \- My activity, Chat, Knowledge base, App info, Support, Offline work, Map, Request
\- \*\*Panic Configuration\*\*: Activates or deactivates a panic button, allowing emergency alerts via email, SMS, or call.
\- \*\*Sitemap Configuration\*\*: Upload an image to provide directions or details about the site.
\- \*\*Breaks\*\*: Time intervals for rest:
&#x20; \- Automatic: Deduct break hours after a set duration
&#x20; \- Manual: Clock in/out breaks, which can be paid or unpaid
&#x20; \- Inactive: Disables breaks entirely

\*\*How to Access Mobile Configurations\*\* &#x20;
1\. \*\*Select\*\* your site in Site Management. &#x20;
2\. \*\*Open\*\* the “Mobile Configuration” tab. &#x20;
3\. \*\*Customize\*\* your preferred options. &#x20;
4\. \*\*Save\*\* changes for each configuration area.

* **Legacy Subscriptions**: Must request new site creation through mobohubb support.
  * **New Subscriptions**: The number of sites you can manage is based on how many mobile app users you have.
