## Understanding “Downloads”

If an export (report, data file, etc.) exceeds size limits or cannot be transferred to your computer right away, Mobohubb places it in **Downloads** as a pending file. Once you have the space or availability, you can **re-download** it at your convenience.

> **Tip:** The **Downloads** section displays relevant info like **format type** (PDF, Excel, etc.), **file name**, and **status**, making it easy to locate and manage large exports.
