**In this guide you'll learn:**

* How to **enable MFA** via phone or app-based token
* How to **deactivate MFA** if you no longer need it
* Important notes on **managing** MFA options in your profile settings

***

# Enabling MFA

1. **Open** the mobohubb portal’s top menu and **click** on **Profile**.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/30187c04eb83a7a801db9d512b1177f4e43bd1375c8e6fa0d6bf9b483bf5c627-Screenshot_2025-05-06_at_5.00.09_PM.png" />

2. **Navigate** to **“Security Settings”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/c48a4d38c8c135ab241086bb31f0c58221bd0fd0ab12f87d912e6301621b83da-image.png" />

<br />

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Phone Number (2FA)" icon="fa-mobile">
    **4. Enable authentication with phone number**

    * This links MFA to the **user’s phone number**.
    * **Click** the button to enable 2-Factor phone authentication.

    ![](https://files.readme.io/cb6d011668fe657fe651e069b3db1652dacdd57d9fc3afca1372dbd04d0b12aa-image.png)

    > **Note:** If you choose this method, you may **skip** the app-based token steps.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="App-Based Token" icon="fa-key">
    **5. Enable authentication with an app-based token**

    * **Click** “Enable” to display a **QR code**.
    * **Scan** this code with your **Microsoft Authenticator** (or similar) app.
    * **Enter** the **token** generated by the authenticator in the empty field.

    ![](https://files.readme.io/897514eef9036d965591f0aef1b6054b182f3211a14993d8335ad0096ea45e62-image.png)
    ![](https://files.readme.io/20f61d4f238f808e1e01741a4aa49b5295ca342570c4fd81dddc5874c2164598-image.png)
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

> **Important:** Only **one method** can be active at a time. If phone-based 2FA is enabled, you cannot enable app-based, and vice versa.

***

## Using MFA at Login

After entering your **username** and **password**, the system prompts you to complete the second factor:

* **Phone**: Enter the **verification code** sent via SMS.
* **App**: Enter the **token** generated by your authenticator app.

***

## Deactivating MFA

If you need to **disable** multi-factor authentication:

1. **Remove** 2FA

   * **Click** **“Remove Two-Factor Authenticator.”**

   <br />

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="80% " src="https://files.readme.io/585ff3a5c00b111fc08cfb00950a88b68e660c673d6dba4e58ea556fd5b0f14e-image.png" />

   Once deactivated, you’ll return to the standard **username + password** login flow.

***

**You’re all set!**

MFA is now successfully integrated into your security routine. Enjoy the added protection for your mobohubb account.
