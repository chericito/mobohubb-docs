### Metadata Description

Learn how to change the **administrator’s portal password** in **mobohubb**. This password is separate from mobile app credentials and differs from other users’ passwords, ensuring that only the administrator can update portal-level access.

<br />

### In This Guide You'll Learn

<br />

* How to **access** the “Profile” section
  * How to **navigate** to the Password Change option
    * How to **update** your administrator portal password securely
      <br />

# Portal Password

<br />

The **portal password** for the **mobohubb** site can be changed in this section. It is distinct from any user’s **mobile app** password or **other users’ portal passwords**, as it belongs solely to the administrator account.

1. **Go** to the portal’s top menu.
2. **Find** and **click** on the **“Profile”** section.
3. This is where you manage your personal information.
4. **Navigate** to **“Password Change.”**
5. \*\*Click\*\* \*\*“Change Password”. \*\* &#x20;

   !\[Password Change Prompt]\(https\://files.readme.io/0d031fc927a3bbc6c944d29d7ba1eaf728e6e7f8bb2bb0c3facf41a6c05b2412-image.png)\{:width="70%"}
6. 5. \*\*Fill in\*\* the fields, including your current password, to authenticate. &#x20;

      !\[Password Fields]\(https\://files.readme.io/cdec6c310021b142ae95f219c1e8db95c859b4be4c5a476157d316c940fdd385-image.png)\{:width="75%"}
      6. **Enter** your new password.
         * Ensure it meets all **requirements** (minimum 6 characters, inclusion of special characters, etc.).
           * **Save** to finalize your updated portal password.

The password for the Mobohubb site can be changed in this section. It should not be confused with the password for users of the mobile app or other user's portal password, as this password is solely used by the administrator.

1. Go to the portal’s top menu.
2. In the top menu, find and click on the “Profile” section. This is where you manage your personal information.
3. Navigate to “Password Change”.
4. Click on "Change Password".

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="70% " src="https://files.readme.io/0d031fc927a3bbc6c944d29d7ba1eaf728e6e7f8bb2bb0c3facf41a6c05b2412-image.png" />

5. To change your password, you must fill in the fields including the current password.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="75% " src="https://files.readme.io/cdec6c310021b142ae95f219c1e8db95c859b4be4c5a476157d316c940fdd385-image.png" />

6. Update your new password. Make sure it meets all requirements such as minimum length (6 characters) or special characters.
