**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to check notifications by opening the notification panel and marking alerts as read.
* How to delete unwanted notifications from the panel.
* How to confirm actions to ensure notifications are properly updated.

***

Notifications in mobohubb serve various purposes, such as informing you about new updates, reminding you of tasks, and alerting you to important events. They are grouped by function (e.g., report, tasks, schedule, portal, and device) to help you quickly locate and manage the information.

## Check Notifications

1. **Verify** that the bell icon at the top of the app shows an indicator.

2. **Tap** the bell icon to open the notification panel.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/b59254a33cd6fe2f63d7af4f428411cd232b07eb54eae80bdf35ed9a9a22bc76-image.png" />

3. **Locate** the new notification within its corresponding section.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/8bf0011c691b5bdc4e3a5557e1063e36aa8234896757ad47d2d4da3cda73a8b1-image.png" />

4. **Swipe right** on the notification to mark it as read.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/a6bc42e79fff285e2bc1a9226aa8f5e0dcf3f5a3fc6d1907e426b9f32da76a20-image.png" />

5. **Confirm** the action by tapping the confirmation button.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/f6f8ed26a8f7f145e77b39630c6f57e9429acb11edb084602dd6a385d8f359bc-image.png" />

6. **Close** the notification interface.

## Delete Notifications

1. **Verify** that the bell icon at the top of the app shows an indicator for new notifications.

2. **Tap** the bell icon to open the notification panel.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/9b4377b25e222a86e9fee22b3e44442e23d4b9d20761554dc8fc2d146fc794a1-image.png" />

3. **Locate** the notification you wish to delete.

4. **Swipe left** on the notification to initiate deletion.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/ae88e3992222d5a7e7a2b677d92b3902baf9685ac9461857527c0601ac0d1dff-image.png" />

5. **Confirm** the deletion by tapping the confirmation button.

   <Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/74a44c4281b180d5d9beeca5980084955b9c8dbce36a1b1ae2b6d9839f45cf66-image.png" />

6. **Close** the notification interface.
