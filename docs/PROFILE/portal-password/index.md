**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **access** the “Profile” section
* How to **navigate** to the Password Change option
* How to **update** your administrator portal password securely

***

The **portal password** for the **mobohubb** site can be changed in this section. It is distinct from any user’s **mobile app** password or **other users’ portal passwords**, as it belongs solely to the administrator account.

1. **Go** to the portal’s top menu.
2. **Find** and **click** on the **“Profile”** section.
3. This is where you manage your personal information.
4. **Navigate** to **“Password Change.”**
5. **Click** on **“Change Password”.**

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="70% " src="https://files.readme.io/0d031fc927a3bbc6c944d29d7ba1eaf728e6e7f8bb2bb0c3facf41a6c05b2412-image.png" />

6. **Fill in** the fields, including your current password, to authenticate.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} width="75% " src="https://files.readme.io/cdec6c310021b142ae95f219c1e8db95c859b4be4c5a476157d316c940fdd385-image.png" />

7. **Enter** your new password.

* Ensure it meets all **requirements** (minimum 6 characters, inclusion of special characters, etc.).
* **Save** to finalize your updated portal password.
