**In this guide you'll learn**

* How to **access** and **edit** your profile information
* How to **modify** your email, phone number, and username
* How to **save** changes to keep your personal data current

***

You can update your personal information in the portal, modifying your **email**, **phone number**, and **username** displayed in the portal.

## How to Update Personal Information

1. **Go** to the portal’s top menu.
2. **Find** and **click** on the **“Profile”** section.
   * This is where you visualize your personal information.
3. **Click** the **“Edit”** button to change the language.
4. **Edit** your personal information and **click** **“Save”** to confirm the changes.
