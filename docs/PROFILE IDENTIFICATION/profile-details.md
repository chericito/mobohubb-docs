**In this guide you'll learn**

* Where to find the **profile details** section
* How to **open** and **scroll** through your user information
* Which personal details are displayed (name, last name, email, etc.)

***

You can view your profile information—like your name, last name, title, email address, and phone number—by tapping on your username in the **Profile Identification** menu.

## Steps to View Your Profile Details

1. **Open** the side menu: Tap the **hamburger button** in the top-left corner of the app.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/8ed3d38f6fbde381a3ce459ee04a5dc92a5baf3b8dd1781ff0298948df34a597-image.png" />

2. **Select** your **profile**: Tap on your username or profile picture to reach the **Profile Identification** screen.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/d2b02befff95b982bafd5da01dfe17dd5f01dba20d740d57831bb056ae9eccc9-image.png" />

3. **Tap** **“My Profile.”**: A new screen will open, showing your personal details.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/dc5ffc7e88108b2512e03c29503b946d386c72a1ac536a31dcf25550bb88d1ea-image.png" />

4. **Scroll** down: Review all information, including name, title, email address, and phone number.

<Image align="center" width="30% " src="https://files.readme.io/ebac113a6cd51aa4b4672b39dc4947688e3c848150f73912e7a61589acea3339-image.png" />
